# Nexus Corp Overview

Nexus Corp is a global technology solutions provider founded in 2018 by Sarah Jenkins and David Lin. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, the company specializes in cloud-native application development, enterprise AI integration, and cybersecurity audits.

## Leadership
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Sarah Jenkins
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO): David Lin
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Marcus Vance

## Core Products
1. NexusCloud: A multi-cloud orchestration platform designed to automate deployment pipelines.
2. CortexAI: An enterprise-grade large language model fine-tuned for automated compliance and legal document review.
3. ShieldNet: A zero-trust network access (ZTNA) security suite.

As of fiscal year 2025, Nexus Corp employs over 1,200 people globally across offices in Austin, London, and Tokyo, and reported an annual revenue of $145 million.
