# Employee Benefits and PTO Policy (2026 Update)

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 20 days of standard PTO per calendar year, credited on a monthly basis.
- Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year; any excess is forfeited on March 31.

## Health and Wellness
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following employment start dates.
- The company offers a wellness stipend of $50 per month, redeemable for gym memberships, fitness applications, or ergonomic home office equipment.
